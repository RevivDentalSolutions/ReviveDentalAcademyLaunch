Revive Academy Brand Guide

Revive Academy should feel premium, calm, and instructional: a modern dental education product rather than a generic slide deck.

Visual principles:

- Dark charcoal backgrounds with subtle depth.
- Aqua accents used sparingly for hierarchy and focus.
- Glassmorphism cards with thin borders and soft glow.
- Faint hexagon accents as a recurring brand motif.
- Outline-style icons only.
- Large readable text for video.
- Strong negative space and consistent margins.
- No bounce, spin, cartoon, neon, or busy decorative effects.
